Privacy policy

We store your name, email, gender, date of birth (to confirm you are 18+), a salted scrypt hash of your password, and the places, trips and reviews you save. We never store your password in plain text and never sell your data. Sign-in uses one essential cookie (sid); we use no tracking or advertising cookies. Weather, maps and photos are requested from Open-Meteo and Wikipedia, which see your IP address. To delete your data, email the site owner.

Terms of use

Reviews must be honest and lawful; we may remove any review. The service is provided as is, without warranty.
